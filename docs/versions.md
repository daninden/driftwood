## 0.36.7 "Memory" — 2026-11-13

Agreed with the user (2026-11-13): the optional AI forgot everything between visits and kept only a short list while the page was open. Now it has a memory that is saved with the island, and it can write notes and a journal. The crab fix of a live session is folded in. With the AI off nothing about the island changes (the rules' scripted lives are unchanged: no decision reads the list and nothing draws a random number).

- **One short-term list, saved.** The chooser's own short-term list (at most 8, not saved: the plan steps it skipped) and `mind.recent` (20) are ONE list: `mind.recent`, the last 20 events and decisions with their outcomes; a skipped plan step is put into it with `mind.remember`. It is what the request calls "What he did lately". It is saved with the island as the optional field `memory` next to `habit` (`src/save` `validMemory`; `ai.memory()` / `ai.restore()` through `main`); an entry that was under way when the page closed comes back as "interrupted". A new game starts empty; no old save is migrated.
- **His own notes.** The plan answer may carry `notes`: the full new list of at most 20 lines of at most 80 characters (cut, whitespace collapsed, empty lines dropped; if there are more than 20 the newest 20 stay), which the model may add to, rewrite or drop; a missing or unusable `notes` keeps the old ones, `[]` clears them. The current notes are in every plan request ("Your notes (you keep these yourself; return the full list in `notes` to change them, leave it out to keep them)"); `FIXED` and the answer format explain it.
- **A journal line at sunset.** `ai.tick()` (called by `main` every frame) asks ONE extra request a day, after sunset (`DUSK`, 19:55), when the AI is on and has a key and the budget allows: one line of at most 140 characters in his own voice about the day (it gets the day's short-term list and the state). Not a decision: Dave never waits, the line is stored when it comes back. The day is marked asked before the answer, so a failed request is not repeated that day, also across a reload. With the AI off, no key or no budget there is no line that day and the rules never make one up. All lines are kept in `memory.journal` ([{ day, line }]); only the last 3 days go into a plan request. The client runs one request at a time, so while the journal request runs a plan request waits (the rules decide, as for any busy client).
- **The AI panel.** A collapsible "Memory" block under the top line shows his notes and the journal (newest day first); its open/closed state is kept in `localStorage` (`driftwood.aiMemoryOpen`, try/catch). Nothing of it is anywhere else in the game.
- **The crab fix (seen live, 09:14: the voice said a stroll to the crabs and he fetched wood).** The crab trip was not possible (the night fire was out and he had no fibre), the chooser said the step's reason first, `to: 'crabs'` was then silently ignored (`toIgnored`) and the game picked another stroll kind (the shore, which picks up driftwood). Now: (1) a place the model named that is not possible makes the step NOT POSSIBLE with the real reason ("no stroll to the crabs now: no fire burning and no fibre to light one"), handled like any impossible pastime step in `follow` (remembered with the reason; a new plan when one can be asked, else skipped), at the goal point before bridging and at the pastime point; (2) the step's reason is said only when the step really starts as planned (`onStep` comes after the check); (3) `crabTripBlock(s)` (game/mind, pure) gives the plain reason from the same checks as `crabTripOk` (not low water, a crab carried, a failed trip a moment ago, outside 04:19 to 15:50, no fire and no way to light one: not able, no fibre, no dry wood), `mind.blockers().crabs` has it (also "he knows no spot with crabs near"), `mind.destinations()` offers the crabs exactly when there is none; the request's destinations line says "Crabs: not now: reason" and the tide line says a stroll to the crabs is possible only when it is (else "low water, but no stroll to the crabs now: reason"; nothing when the game cannot say).
- **The crab trip's hours are a habit (the same principle as 0.36.6).** `crabTripOk`, the menu's condition, is now any low water from `AI_FROM` (04:19) to `AI_TRIP_TO` (15:50) with the fire conditions; `CRAB_FROM` (09:07) to `CRAB_TO` are the rules' own hours in the new `crabHabitOk`, which the rules' choices use (the crab trip first, the rules' stroll kind, `crabDue`, the habit explore). The rules' lives are unchanged (the existing life tests and fixtures pass unchanged); `tests/mind.test.js` `crabTripOk` asserts the new menu hours and `crabHabitOk` the old window. The blocker reason says "outside 04:19 to 15:50".

- **One walk rule** (`game/walk`): every straight line a route uses is checked with the same rule the walking uses (terrain steps as `stepPlayer` takes them, and all solids), so a route never promises what he cannot walk.
- **One planner, two layers** (`game/path`, `game/fine`): the 1.5 m terrain grid is cached again, and a fine layer of nodes round camp solids (piles, fires, the lean-to's inside, opening and corners) is searched with it; it is updated locally when the camp changes.
- **One follower** (`game/route`): for the habit, the player's clicks and the tests; it follows the furthest visible route point, closes exactly the segment it is stuck on and plans again, and says honestly when there is no way. No more hand-steering escapes.
- **Standing points** (`game/stand`): to feed or light a fire, take from or add to a pile, build or upgrade, he walks to a free spot where the game really offers that action on the intended thing.
- **Agreed rules:** the inside of a lean-to and the area just in front of its opening stay free of wood (wood laid there goes beside it; the pieces left over from building lie beside the lean-to, one pile per kind); building is offered from any pile within reach, not only the nearest. A consequence: the drying pile at a lean-to now lies behind it and keeps dry once the walls are built (it can no longer lie under the bare roof).
