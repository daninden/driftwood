## Done and live on `main`

Everything up to 0.16.2 "Light load" is built (the game lowers its own quality on a slow device: five levels from a 2048 px shadow map via a lighter shadow filter and pixel ratio 1 to no terrain shadows, measured after the island fades in, never back up, remembered per device, `qN` in the dev panel). Before that, up to 0.16.1 "Heavy legs" is built (needs slow him clearly: tired down to 60 %, thirsty down to 65 %, hungry down to 75 %, multiplied together and with the load, and his steps get shorter with the slowing). Before that, up to 0.16 "Lean-to" is built (branches under the broadleaf and rain forest trees, carried and piled like driftwood and burning half as long; the lean-to built from a pile of 6 driftwood and a pile of 6 branches by a hold, in steps, kept when interrupted; a longer cold sleep under it and faster rest by a fire; plus a fix: a coconut can always be husked and eaten, also with three tufts, and its button icon is the half coconut). Before that, up to 0.15 "Coconut" is built (hunger from two days without eating, starving from four; husking a coconut takes 4 seconds and he eats the nut: one tuft of fibre, 4/3 day of hunger and a quarter day of thirst less; a half coconut mark next to the drop). Before that, up to 0.14.2 "Still edges" is built (shadows without jagged edges: a 4096 px shadow map on desktop and 2048 px on phones, a soft filter of 3 texels with bilinear taps). Before that, up to 0.14.1 "Dry ground" is built (a first pile never goes in the water, the thumb stick stops kneeling work, a first frame never runs backwards; the smoke test runs on the graphics card). Before that, up to 0.14 "Moving water" is built (slow glints and swell on the sea, streaks and flecks drifting down rivers, moving white water at fords and falls, calmer lagoons, a breathing surf). Before that, up to 0.13.3 "Clear marks" (every mark has a glow in the opposite colour, thicker strokes, a paper disc in the action button, clearer opacities). Before that, up to 0.13.2 "Chosen shadows": the shadows settle on steps of 1/32°, a minimum light height of 15°, soft edges and the tight fit (chosen by the user by eye; the comparison parameter is gone). Before that, up to 0.13.1 "Soft shadows": the shadow map covers only what is seen (texels about 3 times finer on the sand with a low sun), shadow edges have an own soft filter, and the shadow light turned in steps of 1/16°. Before that, up to 0.13 "Action": clicking and tapping only walk, and everything he does goes through one action button (Space, and an ink ring bottom right with a small icon of what a press does; hold it to light a pile or sleep; `F` and `Z` stay as shortcuts), and a full fire takes no more wood. Before that, up to 0.12.1 "Smooth shadows": the shadow light turns in steps of 1/32° (was ¼°, which jerked) and never comes below 15° (was about 11.5°); PCF soft edges are off. Before that, up to 0.12 "Piles": piles hold at most 12 pieces and one kind (a new pile starts right next to a full one), tally marks in ink above a pile and a fire while he stands beside it (`src/render/tallyMarks.js`), wet wood cannot be laid on a fire, the fire's tepee grows and shrinks with its pieces, and the day line and waking line are bigger and clearer with a soft glow. Before that, up to 0.11.1 "Still shadows": the shadows hold still around sunrise and sunset and move in tiny steps as the sun moves (the shadow light's direction is quantised in steps of about ¼°, see `src/render/stage.js`). Before that, up to 0.11 "Water": thirst (thirsty after a day, very thirsty after a day and a half, a collapse at two days), drinking by tapping river water or its bank, the drop mark next to the moon, and the burnt fire place that stays two days. Before that, up to 0.10 "Wet wood": wet wood that looks wet until it will burn (dark, cool, a slight sheen) with a damp patch in the sand, carrying that slows more (12 % a piece) with shorter steps and a stoop, and the view kept per island. Before that, up to 0.9 "Name": the fire and the torch, sleeping by the fire, wading, the inventory row, the name asked at the first start, the day line top left (`Dave, day 1`), the waking line, and the ink turning pale at night. ## Next, agreed with the user

In this order, each with its decisions in `docs/design.md`:

1. **0.17 "Wildlife"** (approved): crabs, fish and birds, atmosphere only. Decisions, to go into *Wildlife* in `docs/design.md`:
   - Crabs: about 12 cm, orange-red body, two claws, little legs; small groups on the beach with a few holes (dark dots in the sand), more out on the wet sand at low water; short sideways runs; within about 3 m they scuttle sideways to the nearest hole and vanish, and come out again after 10 to 20 s once he is more than 5 m away; also at night.
   - Fish: dark elongated shadows in groups of 2 to 5 in shallow river water and lagoons, facing upstream in rivers with a little wiggle; when he wades within about 2 m they dart off and fade, and come back slowly. Now and then (every half to one and a half minutes when the coast is in view) a fish jumps just beyond the surf: a small arc and a splash in the white-water style.
   - Birds: white seabirds with grey wing tips, flapping and gliding. By day 2 to 4 circle high over the coast (no shadows). Every few minutes of play a flock of 5 to 8 lands on the beach 10 to 20 m from him and pecks about; within about 4 m they fly up, circle and leave. In the evening they fly to the rocky shore and are gone; no birds at night.
   - Alive only within about 30 m of him, instanced; moving animals do not count as "something happening" for the 15 fps idle rate; nothing saved; no sound.
2. **0.17.1 "River mouth"** (fix): the river does not meet the sea well. Beyond the mouth its bed runs on into the sea floor as a deep, dark trough with a rounded end, clearly visible through the water (seen on seed 1337 at a mouth with sand on both sides). The river bed should flatten out into the sea floor at the mouth, so the shallows run on smoothly. This changes the island: `tests/world.test.js` guards seed 1337 against the style prototype, so the prototype gets the same change and the fixture is regenerated with `prototypes/make-golden.js` (see `CLAUDE.md`).
3. **0.18 "Low water finds"** (approved): new driftwood washes up in the strip between low and high water instead of on the dry beach, placed at high water while the spot is under water, so you never see it appear; same numbers per stretch of beach as now. At low water it comes out and can be picked up (also standing on wet sand or in shallow water); while its spot is under the sea it cannot be picked up. In shallow water it shows faintly through the water (the water shader does that). Wood in the strip stays wet: every time the water covers it the drying starts over, so you carry it up to dry. Wood left lying stays; the next high water does not take it, it only adds new wood up to the limit per stretch of beach. The start wood on the dry beach stays where it is. Piles in the strip are still taken by the flood.
4. **0.19 "Wind"** (approved): trees move in the wind, in the existing tree and plant shaders (a small shift per vertex), only on the detailed tiles near him. Palm fronds sway most (tips up and down and a little sideways); broadleaf and rain forest crowns rock slowly as a whole with the leaf clumps a little apart; grass and bushes bend a little; trunks stand still, tall palms bend slightly at the top. A calm base with now and then a gust that rolls over the island as a wave (you see it coming: first the trees further off, then the ones near him). The wind direction is fixed per island and turns very slowly over the day. Moderate on the coast, hardly any in the rain forest (sheltered), strongest on the plateau and summit; calmer at night. The leaf shadows move along (the shadow map gets the same displacement); check live that they stay calm. Wind does not count as "something happening" for the 15 fps idle rate.
5. **Lean-to look** (live session with the user, version number when it ships): the lean-to reads as a gate or fence, because its roof slopes away from a camera that looks along it. Try the open side turned about 45° from the camera, fewer and thinner branches leaning on the ridge, and tidy the leftover pieces round it; branches on the ground are too thick (they look like small logs).

## Notes

- Work directly on `main`; every push goes live. Run `npm test`, and for changes that touch render `npm run build && npm run smoke` (quick, about 15 seconds with a graphics card, about 35 s on software drawing: a few shots at 640x400); for visual versions `npm run build && npm run smoke:full` (every shot, about 20 seconds with a card; shots a person looks at are full size). Look at the screenshots.
- `window.__driftwood` (see `src/main.js`) exposes `world`, `game`, `view` (also `view.scene`), `input`, and `pause`, `step`, `draw` for headless checks. `?spawn=x,z` and `?time=t` help to look at one place or time. A river mouth on seed 1337: `?spawn=-77.7,169.4`. A shallow brook with a bank to drink at, 14 m from the start: water at (-77, 180), bank at (-75.8, 180).
- `?quality=N` (0 to 4) forces a quality level and stops the adapting (like `?spawn` and `?time`); `npm run smoke -- --quality=N` runs the smoke test with it. The level the game chose is kept in `localStorage` under `driftwood-quality`; delete that key to start from level 0 again. The user still has to check on the slow laptop where it settles (the `qN` in the dev panel) and whether the shadows look right there.
- The user checks the shadows on the phone after the 0.14.2 push (2048 px map plus the new filter). A cross-fade between two shadow lights was tried live and rejected: the shadows looked doubled during the fade.
- The user writes in Dutch; everything in the repository stays in English.
