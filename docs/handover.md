## Done and live on `main`

Everything up to 0.22.2 "Clearer finds" is built (a failed crab dig shows the crab running off to another hole; driftwood carried shows in the inventory row; the fish are a soft teal instead of black, the instance colours were never filled before, which is the likely reason they were not seen on phones; the camera depth range is 30 to 760; the lean-to's palm fronds lie on the roof). Before that, up to 0.22.1 "Loose ends" is built (rain wets loose wood anywhere on the island, far wood caught up when he comes near, also loose wood after a sleep through a shower; a regrown branch starts dry; wood that washes up during a fast-forward sleep starts drying when the sea last covered its spot; a tile build dropped before it is finished is cancelled and cleaned up; the single-file build has a charset meta; Vitest 5.1.0, `npm audit` clean). Before that, up to 0.22 "Crab catch" is built (crabs can be caught and eaten: beside a hole a crab has just fled into a short press digs for 5 s, about half the digs catch the crab, fixed by the seed and the number of digs, a failed dig sends the crab out of the back so it stays hidden three times as long; at most 2 crabs carried, shown as small crabs in the row and not counting as wood; never eaten raw, beside a fire with flames a short press roasts and eats one in 8 s for 2/3 day of hunger, ahead of taking a torch but after feeding with dry wood; a raw crab goes bad after a game day; a caught crab returns to its colony 3 to 5 days later; the sounds are a scrape and a sizzle; saved in the optional `crabs`; `game/crabs` pure, `tests/crab.test.js`). ## Bugs, later

- Crab catch (0.22): the sounds of digging and roasting were not heard by the maker; the dig icon (a hand over a hole) reads less clearly than the roast icon and may need redrawing; a crab only shows in a hole it fled into, so how often a catch is possible in real play is for the user to judge (the 50 % chance, the 5 s and 8 s, and the 3 to 5 days of regrowing are in `game/crabs`).
- Rain (0.21): palm shadows stay fairly sharp in rain (the sun is only halved), softer or fainter shadows would read more like an overcast sky.
- Rain (0.22.1): the catch-up for far wood and for a shower slept through checks the shelter with the lean-tos that exist at catch-up time, not those of the time of the shower.
- Rain (0.22.1): a shower slept through fully does not wet piles or carried wood (only loose wood is caught up).
- Rain (0.22.1): the day a regrown branch grew (`g` in `game/branches`) is not saved; after a reload rain from before it grew can wet it.
- Low water finds (0.18): 14 of the 49 beach tiles with start wood on seed 1337 have no strip between the tide lines and get no new wood.
- At a river mouth the river's water ribbon meets the sea along a straight diagonal colour edge (seen after 0.17.1 at `?spawn=-82,184`); the two water colours should blend.
- From the review (`docs/review-gpt.md`): measure frame times on a real phone (walking, turning the view, tiles being built; simulation, building and drawing apart, spikes and not only the average). Only then decide on the tile building budget (about 9 + 3 ms per frame now), fixed simulation steps or the shadows; nothing changes in the look without the user.
- From the review: a tile dropped before it is finished is cancelled and disposed since 0.22.1 and no GPU leak was found headless; still worth a look on a real phone during long walks.

## Notes

- Work directly on `main`; every push goes live. Run `npm test`, and for changes that touch render `npm run build && npm run smoke` (quick, about 15 seconds with a graphics card, about 35 s on software drawing: a few shots at 640x400); for visual versions `npm run build && npm run smoke:full` (every shot, about 20 seconds with a card; shots a person looks at are full size). Look at the screenshots.
- `window.__driftwood` (see `src/main.js`) exposes `world`, `game`, `view` (also `view.scene`), `input`, and `pause`, `step`, `draw` for headless checks (`draw` also sets the wind clock to 20 s; `view.setWindTime(t)` sets it by hand). `?spawn=x,z` and `?time=t` help to look at one place or time. A river mouth on seed 1337: `?spawn=-77.7,169.4`. A shallow brook with a bank to drink at, 14 m from the start: water at (-77, 180), bank at (-75.8, 180).
- `game.wildlife` (via `window.__driftwood.game`) lists what is alive near him (`crabs()`, `holes()`, `fish()`, `birds()`, `jump()`), and `callFlock()` and `coloniesNear(x, z, r)` help to look at one thing; the smoke shots `smoke-25.png` (crabs at low water) and `smoke-26.png` (a landed flock) use them.
- `?quality=N` (0 to 4) forces a quality level and stops the adapting (like `?spawn` and `?time`); `npm run smoke -- --quality=N` runs the smoke test with it. The level the game chose is kept in `localStorage` under `driftwood-quality`; delete that key to start from level 0 again. The user still has to check on the slow laptop where it settles (the `qN` in the dev panel) and whether the shadows look right there.
- To see a shower (0.21): `?time=t` takes days too, so `?time=14.56` starts on day 14 just as the second shower of seed 1337 begins (daytime, about 1.5 game hours long, full strength after ten game minutes); `?time=7.98` is the first one (at night). The showers of an island: `createWeather(world.seed).showers(60)` (in the console: `__driftwood.game.weather.showers(60)`); `game.weather.next(game.clock.total)` is the one under way or the next. To force rain at any time in the console: `__driftwood.game.weather.intensity = () => 0.9` (the smoke test does that for its shots). `game.rain` is the intensity now. The user should judge the rain by eye and ear: the streak count and speed (`render/rain`), the grey of the light (`render/mood`, `RAIN_GRADE`, `greyC`), the rings (`rainRings` in `render/water`), the ground (`uWet` in `render/materials`) and the rain levels in `LEVELS` (`rain`, `hiss`). The fire goes out after 15 game minutes of rain (`RAIN_OUT`, 15 s of play): a shower is only 60 to 120 game minutes, so an open fire almost never survives one; say if that is too harsh.
- To try the crabs (0.22): at low water walk up to a colony on the beach (`game.wildlife.coloniesNear(x, z, r)`), the crabs scuttle into holes within 3 m; stand beside one of them (`game.wildlife.hiding(x, z, 0.8)` says which) and press the button. In the console `game.crabs.digs` is the number of digs (it fixes the next dig's luck), `game.crabs.carried` the crabs carried; the smoke shots `smoke-34.png` (dig icon) and `smoke-35.png` (crab in the row, roast icon) use them.
- The user should listen and judge the mix of 0.20 "Sound": it was set by reasoning, never heard by the maker (headless checks only: no page errors, the context exists, M flips it; the rain and hiss of 0.21 likewise). All levels are in one table, `LEVELS` in `audio/mix` (plus `MASTER`); tones are in `audio/sound`. Open questions for the listening: is the surf too loud or too constant, are the crickets, gulls and crackles audible enough (they are the softest), do the footsteps sound right on each ground, and is the touch speaker mark (above the moon, 26 px with a larger tap area) well placed.
- The user wants to check the moving shadows of 0.19 "Wind" live (leaf and frond shadows now sway with the plants; the displacement in the shadow depth pass is on at every quality level). Looked at here: two frames at the same wind time are identical, shadows only move with the plants. If it looks restless, the amplitudes are in `KINDS` in `render/wind`, and the shadow displacement could be switched off per quality level by giving the swaying materials no `userData.depthMat`.
- The user checks the shadows on the phone after the 0.14.2 push (2048 px map plus the new filter). A cross-fade between two shadow lights was tried live and rejected: the shadows looked doubled during the fade.
- The user writes in Dutch; everything in the repository stays in English.
