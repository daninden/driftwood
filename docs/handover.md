Before that, up to 0.14.2 "Still edges" is built (shadows without jagged edges: a 4096 px shadow map on desktop and 2048 px on phones, a soft filter of 3 texels with bilinear taps). Before that, up to 0.14.1 "Dry ground" is built (a first pile never goes in the water, the thumb stick stops kneeling work, a first frame never runs backwards; the smoke test runs on the graphics card). Before that, up to 0.14 "Moving water" is built (slow glints and swell on the sea, streaks and flecks drifting down rivers, moving white water at fords and falls, calmer lagoons, a breathing surf). Before that, up to 0.13.3 "Clear marks" (every mark has a glow in the opposite colour, thicker strokes, a paper disc in the action button, clearer opacities). ## Next, agreed with the user

In this order, each with its decisions in `docs/design.md`:

1. **0.17.1 "River mouth"** (fix): the river does not meet the sea well. Beyond the mouth its bed runs on into the sea floor as a deep, dark trough with a rounded end, clearly visible through the water (seen on seed 1337 at a mouth with sand on both sides). The river bed should flatten out into the sea floor at the mouth, so the shallows run on smoothly. This changes the island: `tests/world.test.js` guards seed 1337 against the style prototype, so the prototype gets the same change and the fixture is regenerated with `prototypes/make-golden.js` (see `CLAUDE.md`).
2. **0.17.2 "Many lights"** (fix, asked by the user): every fire and torch near the castaway lights its surroundings, not only the strongest one. Now `render/fire` uses one point light for the strongest fire or torch near him plus one for the torch in his hand. Use a small fixed pool of point lights (always in the scene, so no shader is rebuilt when one is added; unused ones at intensity 0), given to the nearest burning fires and torches each frame, with the glow over the picture following them. Fewer lights on the lower quality levels of 0.16.2 if they cost too much.
3. **0.17.3 "Fireflies"** (approved): from dusk to an hour before sunrise, groups of 10 to 30 at forest edges and along rivers in the lowland and rain forest (not on the open beach, not on the summit), fixed per island by the seed like the animals. Small warm yellow points with a soft glow, drifting slowly at 0.3 to 2 m, blinking slowly each in its own rhythm, now and then a few flash together. Their light is a soft glow over the picture (like the night glow round fires and torches), not real light on the ground (too costly). They drift aside a little when he walks through and drift back. They do not count as "something happening" for the 15 fps idle rate.
4. **0.18 "Low water finds"** (approved): new driftwood washes up in the strip between low and high water instead of on the dry beach, placed at high water while the spot is under water, so you never see it appear; same numbers per stretch of beach as now. At low water it comes out and can be picked up (also standing on wet sand or in shallow water); while its spot is under the sea it cannot be picked up. In shallow water it shows faintly through the water (the water shader does that). Wood in the strip stays wet: every time the water covers it the drying starts over, so you carry it up to dry. Wood left lying stays; the next high water does not take it, it only adds new wood up to the limit per stretch of beach. The start wood on the dry beach stays where it is. Piles in the strip are still taken by the flood.
5. **0.19 "Wind"** (approved): trees move in the wind, in the existing tree and plant shaders (a small shift per vertex), only on the detailed tiles near him. Palm fronds sway most (tips up and down and a little sideways); broadleaf and rain forest crowns rock slowly as a whole with the leaf clumps a little apart; grass and bushes bend a little; trunks stand still, tall palms bend slightly at the top. A calm base with now and then a gust that rolls over the island as a wave (you see it coming: first the trees further off, then the ones near him). The wind direction is fixed per island and turns very slowly over the day. Moderate on the coast, hardly any in the rain forest (sheltered), strongest on the plateau and summit; calmer at night. The leaf shadows move along (the shadow map gets the same displacement); check live that they stay calm. Wind does not count as "something happening" for the 15 fps idle rate.
6. **0.20 "Sound"** (approved): all sound made in the browser (Web Audio: filtered noise and simple tones), no sound files. Layers, soft and depending on where he is: the surf swelling and falling back in the rhythm of the breathing surf of 0.14 (loud on the coast, faint inland); a river murmur near running water (louder at fords and falls); wind following the gusts and strength per height of 0.19 (rustling in palms, howling on the summit); fire and torch crackle nearby; footsteps on dry sand, wet sand, water and grass following his steps (also the heavy steps of 0.16.1); the work (husking, drilling, building); gulls when a flock is near; crickets in the forest at night. `M` turns sound on or off; on touch devices a small ink sound mark next to the moon; the choice is remembered per device. Sound starts after the first key or tap (browsers require it; answering the name question counts).
7. **0.21 "Rain"** (approved): about once a week: the first shower 5 to 9 days in, and each next one 5 to 9 days after the last, fixed per island by the seed; never in the first two days. A shower lasts one to two game hours (1 to 2 minutes of play): greyer, softer light, thin streaks of rain, rings on the water, a slight sheen on the ground, and rain in the sound of 0.20. Wood in the open gets wet (loose pieces, piles and what he carries) and has to dry again; wood within about 1.5 m of a lean-to stays dry; branches get wet too but dry faster than driftwood. A fire in the open burns down faster and goes out hissing after a few minutes of rain, leaving wet ash; a fire within 2 m of a lean-to keeps burning. Torches burn twice as fast in the rain.
8. **0.22 "Crab catch"** (approved): at a hole a crab just fled into, the action button makes him kneel and dig (about 5 s); about half the time he has the crab. More crabs at low water, so catching is easier then. He carries at most 2 crabs (a small crab in the row). He does not eat them raw: beside a burning fire a short press makes him roast and eat one (about 8 s), taking 2/3 day of hunger away (half a coconut). A raw crab not roasted within a day goes bad and is gone. A colony caught empty grows back over a few days. Crabs carried and emptied colonies are saved.

## Notes

- Work directly on `main`; every push goes live. Run `npm test`, and for changes that touch render `npm run build && npm run smoke` (quick, about 15 seconds with a graphics card, about 35 s on software drawing: a few shots at 640x400); for visual versions `npm run build && npm run smoke:full` (every shot, about 20 seconds with a card; shots a person looks at are full size). Look at the screenshots.
- `window.__driftwood` (see `src/main.js`) exposes `world`, `game`, `view` (also `view.scene`), `input`, and `pause`, `step`, `draw` for headless checks. `?spawn=x,z` and `?time=t` help to look at one place or time. A river mouth on seed 1337: `?spawn=-77.7,169.4`. A shallow brook with a bank to drink at, 14 m from the start: water at (-77, 180), bank at (-75.8, 180).
- `game.wildlife` (via `window.__driftwood.game`) lists what is alive near him (`crabs()`, `holes()`, `fish()`, `birds()`, `jump()`), and `callFlock()` and `coloniesNear(x, z, r)` help to look at one thing; the smoke shots `smoke-25.png` (crabs at low water) and `smoke-26.png` (a landed flock) use them.
- `?quality=N` (0 to 4) forces a quality level and stops the adapting (like `?spawn` and `?time`); `npm run smoke -- --quality=N` runs the smoke test with it. The level the game chose is kept in `localStorage` under `driftwood-quality`; delete that key to start from level 0 again. The user still has to check on the slow laptop where it settles (the `qN` in the dev panel) and whether the shadows look right there.
- The user checks the shadows on the phone after the 0.14.2 push (2048 px map plus the new filter). A cross-fade between two shadow lights was tried live and rejected: the shadows looked doubled during the fade.
