The player's **own key**, one connection in the OpenAI-compatible chat format (address, key, model as settings), starting with **one model** (Google AI Studio, a free Gemma or Gemini model; a fallback list of models was discussed and left for later), kept in `localStorage` apart from the save and never shown. The memory (`memory`: short, long, journal) goes in the save per island next to `habit`, a new game starts with none. Answers are structured JSON checked against the menu. The dev panel shows on/off, requests today, the last error and the last request and answer. **Local tests never use AI** (a fixed fake mind, no network, no credits). ## Open

- **Slower days since 0.34.0 "Gestures":** the longer picks and drinks cost Dave time (pick-up and lay 0.55 s to 1.5 s each, a load of three pieces about 4 s more). Measured on the 24 ten-day lives: lean-to by day 10 in 19 of 24 (20), mean day 4.37 (3.90); home complete (bed) by day 10 in 12 (15); no collapse, bounds held. A 2 s bend was measured too and cost more (lean-to mean day 5.11, home complete 9 of 24, `stuck` 5 to 16), so 1.5 s was kept. If the days should be quicker again, the lever is `PICK_TIME` in `game/game` (and `STOOP` is the short bend for what shows no icon).
- **Planner limits left after 0.33.3 "Routes":** (1) 2.2% of 1635 walks still get no plan; of the 36, 33 are unreachable on a 0.5 m lattice anyway (targets in thin pockets), 3 reachable. The two thin pockets near (-96, 99) on seed 1337 are known. (2) Planning cost on long routes: 1.5 to 2.2x (300 m cold about 262 to 400 ms mean). (3) Failing searches take up to about 4 s at worst (3.5 s before). `JOURNEY_RIM` stays as it is. Following is reliable where a plan exists (arrived 100%).
- **Small stalls left after 0.33.3 "Routes":** (1) Arrival stalls 0.2 m short of a standing point (goals fire and stores): the 60-day lives show more `stuck` (2/6/3 to 8/14/6), almost all of this kind, varying with the trajectory. (2) Seed 7 `nopath` 1 to 5: starts boxed in by trunks (not routes). (3) `no-stand: add` at a crowded ring middle (varies 0 to 76 between settings; seed 1337 60 days 21), a camp-layout question. (4) New: `no-stand: buildIn`, the mind's next kind and the game's offer disagree about the held piece (he holds a branch, the game offers only lay); also at 0.33.2 in 5 of 24 ten-day lives.
- **Reach (0.30.16): "home before dark" is still the limit of the day trips** (see the next point).
